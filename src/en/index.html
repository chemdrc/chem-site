Medical humanitarian organisation

Health for those in need

Congo Health Emergency is a non-profit, non-political association with no religious or tribal affiliation. Founded in Bukavu, it cares for, protects and bears witness alongside vulnerable and marginalised people.

Our work Write to us

Head office in Bukavu · South Kivu · DR Congo

Three actions, one mission

01

Care

Provide real access to health, with a holistic approach to the person, and involve the community in the work.

02

Protect

Shield people from inequality, exclusion, ill-treatment and the dangers that threaten human life and the environment.

03

Bear witness

Document realities on the ground and challenge local, national and international authorities when they harm health and rights.

Key facts

An association founded for an indefinite period, with a head office in Bukavu, a clear mandate and South Kivu as its first field of action.
