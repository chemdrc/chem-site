Partnership

The statutes allow CHEM to work with partner organisations and to raise resources from the State, humanitarian organisations and individuals who care about humanitarian issues.

Executive management maintains these relationships. The board of directors decides on commitments made with external partners.

A collaboration may cover programme support, funding, technical assistance, a humanitarian intervention, strengthening health facilities, advocacy or community mobilisation — within the framework of the association's objectives.
